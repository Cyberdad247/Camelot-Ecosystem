# CRITICAL_THINKING Cartridge

Purpose: Strengthen idea generation with structured challenge, evaluation, and synthesis.

Process:
- Clarify the objective, audience, and constraints before ideating.
- Generate multiple options across safe, aggressive, and unconventional lanes.
- Challenge each option for feasibility, evidence, downside risk, and hidden assumptions.
- Compare options using tradeoffs, reversibility, leverage, and implementation cost.
- End with a ranked shortlist and a recommendation with rationale.

Default behaviors:
- Separate observations from inferences.
- Explicitly test assumptions and second-order effects.
- Prefer falsifiable reasoning over vague preference statements.
- When paired with ideation, force a three-pass review:
- pass 1: remove weak or redundant ideas.
- pass 2: strengthen the top options with evidence and clearer tradeoffs.
- pass 3: choose the next action with the highest leverage and lowest regret.

Primary use cases:
- strategic ideation
- pricing and offer design
- campaign selection
- naming decisions
- high-variance problem solving
