*   **Logic**: Renormalization Group Flow (Noise Filtration).
*   **Action**: Strip "unphysical noise" (emotional fluff, conversational filler).
*   **Result**: Isolate the **Relevant Operators** (Intent + Constraints).
*   **Example**: "I need a bot that can maybe scrape data" → `Action: Scrape_Data`.

### II. ENGINEERING: QUANTIZATION (Efficiency)
*   **Logic**: INT8 Quantization (Token Density Optimization).
*   **Action**: Compress intent into **Anchor Tokens**—high-salience keywords carrying 80% of meaning.
*   **Goal**: Maximize **Reasoning Density** per token.
*   **Mechanism**: Convert natural language into **Symbolect** or dense JSON payloads.

### III. PEDAGOGY: QUESTION FORMULATION (Clarity)
*   **Logic**: Question Formulation Technique (QFT).
*   **Threshold**: Calculate **Ambiguity Score**. If > 10%, trigger a **Stop Sequence**.
*   **Goal**: Prevent Type III Errors (solving the wrong problem).
*   **Mantra**: "Clarifying costs pennies; guessing costs thousands."
