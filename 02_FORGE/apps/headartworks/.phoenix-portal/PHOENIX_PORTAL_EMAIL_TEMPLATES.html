3. Shopify Flow Configuration

Navigate to: headartwork.myshopify.com/admin/flow

Create a single flow with the following structure:

TRIGGER: Customer created | CONDITION: Customer tags CONTAINS "phoenix_lead" | +---> ACTION: Send email "Phoenix - Skeleton Key Delivered" | +---> WAIT: 24 hours | +---> ACTION: Send email "Phoenix - 300 Year Deception" | +---> WAIT: 48 hours | +---> ACTION: Send email "Phoenix - Frequency of Awakening" | +---> WAIT: 48 hours | +---> ACTION: Send email "Phoenix - Master Locksmith" | +---> WAIT: 48 hours | +---> ACTION: Send email "Phoenix - Forge Your Reality"
Note: Create all 5 email templates in Shopify Email (/admin/email) BEFORE building the flow. The flow references templates by name.

10. Testing & Launch Checklist

Pre-Launch

  • Create all 5 email templates in Shopify Email
  • Apply gold/black design theme to each template
  • Verify download link works: delivery.shopifyapps.com/-/4ac1dc16f87c78e1/95ddb592359f8513
  • Verify YouTube playlist link works
  • Verify YouTube interview link works
  • Verify headartworks.com link works
  • Verify invisionedmarketing.agency link works
  • Build Shopify Flow automation with all 5 steps + wait timers
  • Send test email from each template to yourself
  • Check email renders on mobile (Gmail, Apple Mail)
  • Check email renders on desktop (Gmail, Outlook)
  • Verify unsubscribe link works

Launch Test

  • Create test customer in Shopify admin with tag "phoenix_lead"
  • Verify Email 1 sends immediately
  • Verify Flow shows "Running" in execution history
  • After 24h: verify Email 2 received
  • After 72h: verify Email 3 received
  • After 120h: verify Email 4 received
  • After 168h: verify Email 5 received
  • Remove test customer or add "test" tag

Live Verification

  • Submit Phoenix Portal lead form with real email
  • Confirm Shopify customer created with "phoenix_lead" tag
  • Confirm Email 1 arrives in inbox (not spam)
  • Confirm download link in email works
  • Monitor Shopify Flow for errors over first 7 days
